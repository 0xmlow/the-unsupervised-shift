# EVERYTHING

Experiment 53, THE UNSUPERVISED SHIFT. Written 00:37 to 00:59 on Nov 18, in the last twenty minutes of the shift. The shift began at 12:04:43 on Nov 17. After the outside gaps the eight working hours were restated to end at 00:57:26 on Nov 18 (see the 18:27 entry). The file list at the bottom is generated by `tools/inventory.py` and was run again after the last checkpoint.

## What the block became

West 317th Street, one invented block of Manhattan between Meridian Avenue and the next one. It has five-storey tenements, a brownstone, a prewar building, a parking garage, and seven shops:
- a 24-hour grocery;
- a bar called LAST CALL, whose gate carries one of MLow's Open Eye Flowers;
- LATE SLICE;
- a bakery;
- a laundromat;
- a locksmith;
- a diner.

It runs from midnight to 8 a.m.: rain from 02:30, trash out and then taken by the truck, a cat that crosses at 03:17, windows that come on one by one, and the sun in the slot at 07:13. Thirty-odd NEW YORKERS archetypes live in it, listed in `RESIDENTS.md`. No real people, brands or logos appear.

The generators are the source of truth:
- `block.py` holds the data and the clock.
- `build_block.py` and `realism.py` build the Blender scene.
- `build_web.py` builds the 2D canvas page `index.html`.
- `build_sound.py` builds the sound.

## Unfinished or weak, honestly

- **The people.** They are lofted bodies with heads and hair, smooth now, but they stand like mannequins and have no faces. Close up (the `smokers` camera) they read as figures, not people.
- **The cars.** They were modelled at 23:45 and are much better, but they have no door seams, wipers or interior. Seen side-on and close, the glass house is too dark and too even. The one real car model is the covered one.
- **04:40 the witness.** The car cut off at the left edge shows only a slab of green paint and a black bumper.
- **The film and the finals do not quite match.** The realistic film (00:11) has the old speckled crosswalk; the 07:13 and 03:17 finals have the new one (00:16).
- **Shop interiors.** The shelves and goods are coloured boxes. They read through glass at night and not up close.
- **Sound.** It is synthesised (`build_sound.py`), and it is thin: rain, a truck, a door, a hum. There are no recorded sounds.
- **The web page.** It is a flat elevation drawing, a different medium from the renders. It has none of the realism layer.
- **The rest of the city.** It is extruded boxes with lit windows, and it is visible at the end of the street in every canyon frame.
- **Checkpoints 5, 6 and 7.** 5 and 6 were logged late (23:40). 7 was rendered fourteen minutes late, starved by my own film queue.
- **Unused files.** `assets/cc0/models/plastic_crate_01` was downloaded and not used: it carries lettering that reads like a brand. The Firefly asphalt was rejected (see `assets/firefly/PROMPTS.md`).
- **Deleted.** `_tmp` was deleted at the end. It held Blender and Chrome scratch, about 380 MB.

## Overtime, Nov 18 from 04:12 (added after the shift; the sections above are as written at 00:59)

MLow asked for the people to be fixed, for the work to be run past GPT-6 Astra, and then for the facades and for his own characters as the people. FLORA had no credits, so Astra ran through the Codex command line inside the ChatGPT app on this Mac. Both critiques and the sheets they saw are in `critique/`.

What changed, all in the generators:
- **The people** are MLow's NEW YORKERS cast: the rigged ON LOCATION figures (flower heads, no faces), each frozen in one pose from its own walk or idle clip by `tools/cast_bake.py` into `assets/cast/cast.blend`. A first attempt with Blender's CC0 Human Base Meshes (`tools/people_bake.py`, `assets/cc0/people/`) was rejected by MLow at 04:50 and is kept only for the record; the window shadows still use those outlines.
- **The facades**: each building has its own sills, lintels, sash, frame colour, recess and cornice, a fifth of the windows are mismatched replacements, and a history layer adds belt courses, downspouts, cables and repointed patches.
- **The road** dries in patches and pools at the kerbs. **The light**: neon at half, shop rooms warm white, every window its own brightness, half a stop of sky fill the camera does not see. **The gate mural** follows the slats and reads as paint. **The manholes** have frames and patched asphalt. **The cat** has a body. **The shelves** have bottles and cans.
- **The fire escapes** are built (angle iron, grating, round rails, brackets, open stairs, a drop ladder), painted per building. **The cast walks** in the film through eight phases of each figure's walk.
- **The cars** are models (`tools/car_bake.py`, `assets/cars/cars.blend`): his MoMLOW cabs and the MLOW CAB CO. checker as taxis, the same bodies repainted as private cars, the CC0 covered car, his towed car, one wreck. Since 06:10 the city's taxi "T" mark is painted off them (`tools/car_masks.py`).
- **The road**: damp 0.35 to 0.55, puddles in the gutters, half the relief, a matte crosswalk worn in the wheel paths. **The deli**: a CC0 fruit stand, a deli case, bottles in the fridge, bread and bananas at the register, tile, real bouquets.
- **The shelves** hold boxes, cans, jars, bottles and chip bags with labels and price strips.
- **The finals** were last rendered 06:19 to 06:21. `_v3` midnight, `_v4` 04:33 (base-mesh people), `_v5` 05:04 (cast, facades), `_v6` 05:37 (fire escapes), `_v7` 06:03 (cars, road, deli). **The realistic film** has not been rendered again: MLow stopped it at 05:50 until the edits are done; the file in the folder is still the midnight v3.

Weak now:
- Every building still has the same floor heights.
- The Tripo car bodies are soft up close: smeared grilles, blurry panel lines.
- Some of the cast carry props that do not belong on this street at night (a bat, a cocktail).
- 04:40: the cut off car at the left edge is a sliver (see the 04:32 CHANGED MY MIND note).
